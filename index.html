A little about me

I'm Ronie Arnold, a multimedia storyteller with a passion for human-interest stories and meaningful communication. I'm drawn to the experiences behind everyday moments and the people whose stories deserve to be heard.

My work brings together writing, photography, videography, graphic design and editorial storytelling. Whether I'm documenting an event, conducting an interview or developing a visual concept, I aim to communicate with clarity, creativity and purpose.

I believe good storytelling goes beyond simply sharing information. It creates understanding, builds connections and gives people a reason to pay attention.

Experience at AMPATH Public Health

My three-month attachment with AMPATH Public Health gave me the opportunity to put communication and storytelling skills into practice in a professional public health environment.

Working with the Communication Unit, I contributed to photography, videography, interviews, transcription, graphic design, video editing and editorial work. I also supported the documentation of community activities and public health programmes.

The experience strengthened my approach to visual storytelling, working with people and communicating information responsibly. It also showed me how thoughtful communication can help make important stories more accessible.
