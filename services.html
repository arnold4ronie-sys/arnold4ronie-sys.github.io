What I do

Ideas, experiences and stories brought to life.

My work brings together different forms of communication and creative production. From written stories and visual documentation to digital content and design, I approach each assignment with attention to detail, purpose and an understanding of its audience.

Skills and services

01. Writing and Editorial

I develop written content that communicates ideas and experiences clearly. My work includes human-interest stories, feature articles, interviews, blog content and transcription. I also provide editorial support through proofreading, grammar correction, document review and improvements to content structure and presentation.

02. Photography and Visual Storytelling

I use photography to document people, places, events and everyday experiences. My practical work includes field photography, portraits and documentation of community and public health activities. I pay attention to composition, lighting and visual perspective to produce meaningful images.

03. Videography and Video Editing

I capture interviews, events and field activities through video. My editing work involves selecting relevant footage, arranging sequences, improving audio, adding captions and preparing finished videos for reports and digital platforms.

04. Graphic Design and Visual Communication

I create visual communication materials, including posters, brochures and awareness designs. Using Canva, I work with typography, colour, layout and visual hierarchy to make information clear, engaging and appropriate for its intended audience.

05. Interviews and Field Documentation

I conduct and document interviews with professionals, community members and beneficiaries to gather firsthand perspectives. This includes audio recording, transcription, photography, videography and documenting activities in field settings. I aim to represent people's experiences accurately and respectfully.

06. Digital Communication and Content Development

I have explored website content development, social media management, content planning and audience engagement. My experience also includes search engine optimisation concepts for Google and YouTube, helping digital content become easier to discover.

How I work

I believe good creative work starts with understanding the assignment. I take time to consider the purpose, audience and message before choosing how best to communicate it.

I value preparation, collaboration, accuracy and careful execution. Whether working independently or alongside a team, I aim to deliver work that meets the brief and serves its purpose.
